# Account Help

## Creating an account
You can create an account with your mobile number or email address. You can also check out as a guest without an account, but you need an account to track orders easily, save addresses, use store credit and earn loyalty points.

## Resetting your password
Go to Login > Forgot password and enter your registered email or mobile number. You will receive a reset link by email or an OTP by SMS. The reset link expires in 30 minutes. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder and request a new one after 2 minutes.

Your new password must be at least 8 characters long and include a letter and a number. You can't reuse your last 3 passwords.

## Account locked after failed logins
After 5 wrong password attempts in a row, your account is locked for 30 minutes to protect it. You can wait, or reset your password straight away using Forgot password, which unlocks the account.

## Logging in with OTP
You can log in with a one-time password sent to your registered mobile number instead of a password. Never share this OTP with anyone, including people who say they are from UnoCX.

## Changing your email or mobile number
Go to Account > Profile > Edit. You need to verify the new email or mobile number with an OTP. If you no longer have access to your old number, contact support with your order history for verification.

An email address or mobile number can be linked to only one UnoCX account. If the new one is already used by another account, you'll need to remove it from that account first.

## Saved addresses
You can save up to 10 delivery addresses in Account > Addresses and choose one at checkout. Editing a saved address doesn't change the address of orders already placed. To change the address on an order that hasn't been dispatched, use My Orders.

## Keeping your account secure
- Use a password you don't use on other websites.
- Never share login or payment OTPs, your password, or your UPI PIN.
- Log out on shared devices.
- If you notice an order or login you don't recognise, reset your password immediately and contact our support team.

## Deleting your account
To delete your account, go to Account > Settings > Delete account. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Pending orders and returns must be completed first. Store credit and loyalty points are lost when the account is deleted.

You can cancel a deletion request by logging in within those 30 days. After deletion, we keep only the order and invoice records that the law requires us to keep.

## Your personal data
You can see and edit your profile details in Account > Profile. To get a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or to ask us to correct it, contact our support team. We respond to data requests within 30 days. We never sell your personal data.

## Order history
All your orders, including cancelled and returned ones, are listed in My Orders. Orders placed as a guest can be linked to your account by signing up with the same email or mobile number.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order from My Orders > Cancel before it is dispatched. After dispatch, you cannot cancel the order, but you can refuse the delivery or return it after delivery. Prepaid cancellations are refunded within 5–7 business days.

You can cancel individual items in an order, as long as they haven't been dispatched. If cancelling an item makes the order total fall below the minimum for a coupon or free shipping, the remaining items keep the price you paid. Cancelling a Cash on Delivery order has no charge.

## Orders cancelled by UnoCX
Occasionally we have to cancel an order, for example if an item is out of stock after ordering, the pincode isn't serviceable, or a payment couldn't be verified. We inform you by SMS and email, and prepaid orders are refunded in full within 5–7 business days.

## Email and SMS preferences
To stop marketing emails, use the Unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email or go to Account > Notifications. Order and delivery updates cannot be turned off.

You can turn WhatsApp order updates on or off in Account > Notifications. Marketing messages on WhatsApp are sent only if you opted in.

## Wishlist and back-in-stock alerts
Tap the heart icon on any product to save it to your Wishlist. Tap Notify me on a sold-out size to get an email when it's back in stock. Wishlist items are not reserved, so they can sell out.

## Loyalty points
You earn 1 UnoCX point for every ₹100 spent on delivered orders. 100 points are worth ₹50 off a future order. Points expire 12 months after they are earned and cannot be exchanged for cash.

Points are added 7 days after delivery, once the return window has closed. If you return an item, the points for it are removed. You can use points to pay for up to 20% of an order's value, and they can be combined with a coupon.
