# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
You can return most items within 7 days of delivery. The 7 days are counted from the delivery date shown in your order tracking, not from the order date. For example, if your order was delivered on 10 March, the last day to raise a return is 17 March. Items must be unworn, unwashed and have all original tags attached. Start a return from My Orders > Select item > Return. A pickup is scheduled within 1–2 business days of your request.

Returns are accepted for orders placed on the UnoCX website and app. Items bought through other marketplaces or retail partners must be returned to that seller, under their rules.

After the 7-day window closes, the Return button disappears from My Orders and we cannot accept a return, except for damaged, defective or wrong items reported within 48 hours of delivery (see below).

## Condition of returned items
Returned items must be in the same condition in which you received them:
- Unworn and unwashed, apart from trying them on.
- All original tags attached, including the brand tag and price tag.
- Free from stains, perfume, deodorant, make-up, pet hair and smoke smell.
- Returned with any free gift, accessory or belt that came with the item.
- Packed in the original product packaging where possible.

Please try clothes on over your innerwear and remove make-up first. Items that don't meet these conditions fail the quality check and are sent back to you without a refund.

## How to start a return
1. Go to My Orders and select the item you want to return.
2. Tap Return, choose a reason and add photos if the item is damaged or wrong.
3. Choose your refund method: original payment method, store credit, or bank account / UPI ID for Cash on Delivery orders.
4. Confirm your pickup address. You will get a return ID by SMS and email.
5. Keep the item packed and ready for the courier.

You can cancel a return request from My Orders any time before the item is picked up.

## Return pickup
A pickup is scheduled within 1–2 business days of your request. The courier makes up to 2 pickup attempts. If both attempts fail, the return request is cancelled and you can raise it again, as long as you are still within the 7-day window.

The courier checks the item, its tags and the quantity at your door, and may refuse the pickup if the item is clearly used or tags are missing. Always take the pickup receipt or confirmation SMS from the courier.

## Pincodes without return pickup
Some remote pincodes don't have reverse pickup. If yours doesn't, the app will show this when you raise the return. You can then send the item yourself to our returns warehouse within 7 days of delivery using any reliable courier. Upload the courier receipt in My Orders, and we reimburse your courier cost up to ₹100 as store credit once the item passes the quality check.

## Items that cannot be returned
Innerwear, swimwear, earrings, face masks and gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged for hygiene reasons. Customised or altered items are also not returnable. Items marked "Non-returnable" on the product page cannot be returned either.

If a non-returnable item arrives damaged, defective or wrong, you can still report it within 48 hours of delivery and we will replace it or refund it.

## Sale items
Items bought during a sale (marked "Final Sale" or bought at 40% off or more) can be exchanged for a different size within 7 days but cannot be refunded. Items bought at less than 40% off follow the normal return policy.

## Return pickup fee
A return pickup fee of ₹99 is deducted from your refund. There is no pickup fee for exchanges or for damaged or wrong items. If you return several items from the same order in one pickup, the ₹99 fee is charged only once.

## Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or colour of the same product within 7 days of delivery, subject to stock. If the new size is out of stock, we refund you instead. Each order item can be exchanged once.

The replacement is dispatched as soon as the original item is picked up, so you don't have to wait for the quality check. If the original item later fails the quality check, the replacement order is cancelled if it hasn't shipped, or the item is sent back to you.

You cannot exchange an item for a different product. To get a different product, return the item for a refund and place a new order.

## Refund timelines
Refunds are started after the returned item passes a quality check at our warehouse, usually within 2 business days of pickup. Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the quality check. UPI refunds are often faster, within 2–3 business days.

You will get an email and SMS when your refund is started, with a refund reference number (ARN or UTR). Your bank may take a little longer to show it on your statement.

## How refunds are paid
- Card, UPI, net banking and wallet payments are refunded to the same account or card you paid with.
- Store credit is added to your UnoCX account instantly after the quality check, if you chose it instead.
- The part of an order paid with a gift card is refunded to that gift card, or as store credit if the gift card has expired.
- Loyalty points used on the order are re-credited to your account.
- Cash on Delivery orders are refunded as described in the next section.

We cannot refund to a different card, bank account or person than the one used to pay, except for Cash on Delivery orders.

## Refunds for Cash on Delivery orders
For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, we refund to your bank account or UPI ID, which you share when you raise the return. You can also choose UnoCX store credit, which is added instantly after the quality check and is valid for 12 months.

The bank account holder's name should match the name on the order. If the details you share are wrong and the transfer fails, we will ask you to update them, and the 5–7 business day timeline starts again from the update.

## Shipping and COD fees
The ₹79 shipping charge and the ₹49 COD handling fee are not refunded when you return an item. They are refunded in full if the item was damaged, defective or wrong, or if you cancel the whole order before it is dispatched.

## Offers, coupons and free gifts
If an order used a coupon and you return only some items, the coupon discount is shared across the items, and your refund is the amount you actually paid for the returned item.

For "Buy X, Get Y" offers, the free item must be returned along with the paid item. If you keep the free item, its full price is deducted from the refund. Free gifts with purchase must also be returned, or their value is deducted.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
If you receive a damaged, defective or wrong item, report it within 48 hours of delivery from My Orders > Report an issue, with photos of the item and the packaging. We will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement or a full refund, including any shipping fee you paid.

Examples include a torn seam, stain, broken zip, missing button, a different size or colour from what you ordered, or a different product. An unboxing video is not required, but it helps us resolve the issue faster.

## Missing items
If your package arrives with an item missing, report it within 48 hours of delivery from My Orders > Report an issue, with a photo of the package and shipping label. Some orders are shipped in more than one package, so check your tracking first. We will investigate with the courier within 3–5 business days and then send the missing item or refund it.

## Tampered or open packages
If the outer package looks opened, torn or tampered with at the time of delivery, please refuse the delivery and contact our support team. Prepaid orders that are refused for this reason are refunded in full.

## Refund rejected after quality check
If a returned item fails the quality check (for example, it is worn, washed, or tags are missing), we send it back to you and no refund is issued. You will receive an email with photos explaining why.

If you disagree with the decision, reply to that email within 7 days and our team will review the photos again. If the item is sent back to you and the delivery fails or you refuse it, we hold it for 30 days before it is donated.

## Refund not received
If the refund timeline has passed and you can't see the money:
1. Check your email or SMS for the refund reference number (ARN or UTR).
2. Share that reference with your bank. It lets them trace the payment.
3. If your bank can't find it, contact our support team with your order number and we will check with our payment partner.

## Returning a gift
If you received an item as a gift, you can return it with the order number. The refund goes to the person who paid for it. If you'd like to keep the value yourself, choose store credit, which is added to your own UnoCX account.
