# Shipping and Delivery

## Where we ship
UnoCX currently ships only within India, to over 19,000 pincodes. We do not ship internationally at the moment. You can check whether we deliver to your pincode on any product page.

We cannot deliver to PO boxes, military APO/FPO addresses, or addresses outside India, even if you pay with an Indian card. If your pincode isn't serviceable, you can use the address of a friend, family member or office in a serviceable pincode.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more. Orders below ₹999 have a shipping charge of ₹79. The ₹999 limit is checked on the order total after discounts and before the COD handling fee.

If one order is shipped in more than one package, you pay the shipping charge only once.

## Order confirmation
After you place an order, you receive an order confirmation by SMS, email and WhatsApp (if you opted in) with your order number. If you don't receive it within 30 minutes, check My Orders in your account. If the order isn't listed, it wasn't placed. Our Payments policy explains what happens to any amount charged.

For some Cash on Delivery orders, we send a confirmation message asking you to confirm the order. Unconfirmed COD orders are cancelled after 24 hours.

## Dispatch time
Orders placed before 2 PM IST on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM, or on Sundays and public holidays, are dispatched the next business day.

During big sales and festive seasons (such as Diwali and end-of-season sales), dispatch can take 1–2 extra business days. We show this on the website during those periods.

## Delivery timelines: how long delivery takes
Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune): 2–4 business days after dispatch.
Rest of India: 4–7 business days after dispatch.
North-East states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep: 7–10 business days after dispatch.
We do not offer same-day or express delivery at the moment.

The delivery date shown at checkout and in tracking is an estimate from the courier, not a guarantee. Heavy rain, floods, strikes, local restrictions and public holidays can cause delays beyond these timelines.

## Orders shipped in more than one package (received only some items)
Received only some of the items in your order? The rest are probably on the way in another package. Items in one order may be shipped from different warehouses and arrive on different days. You will get a separate tracking link for each package. My Orders shows which items are in which package.

## Tracking your order
Once your order is dispatched, you get a tracking link by SMS and email. You can also track it from My Orders > Track. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first update. Our courier partners are Delhivery, Blue Dart and Ekart.

## What tracking statuses mean
- Order placed: we have received your order and payment.
- Packed: your items are packed and waiting for the courier.
- Shipped: the courier has collected your package.
- In transit: the package is moving between courier hubs.
- Out for delivery: the courier will try to deliver it today.
- Delivered: the courier has marked the package as delivered.
- Delivery attempt failed: the courier couldn't deliver it and will try again.
- Returned to origin (RTO): the package is coming back to us after failed delivery attempts.

## Changing your delivery address
You can change the delivery address from My Orders before the order is dispatched. After dispatch, the address cannot be changed. If you won't be available, you can refuse the delivery and the order will be returned to us; prepaid orders are then refunded in full.

You can also change the phone number for delivery updates before dispatch. After dispatch, the courier may let you reschedule the delivery from their tracking page, but they cannot change the address.

## Delivery OTP
For some deliveries, the courier asks for a 4-digit delivery OTP sent to your phone, to confirm the package reached the right person. It is safe to share this delivery OTP with the courier at your door. Never share a payment OTP, card PIN or UPI PIN with anyone, including couriers.

## Receiving your order
Please check the outer package at the door. If it looks opened, torn or tampered with, refuse the delivery and contact our support team. Someone else at your address (family member, security guard, office reception) can receive the package on your behalf.

## Delayed or stuck orders
If your tracking hasn't updated for more than 3 days, or the delivery date has passed, contact our support team with your order number and we will check with the courier. If the courier confirms the package is lost, we send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## Order shows delivered but not received
If tracking says "Delivered" but you haven't received the package:
1. Check with family members, neighbours, your building's security or reception.
2. Wait up to 24 hours. Couriers sometimes mark a package delivered a little early.
3. If you still don't have it, report it within 48 hours of the "Delivered" status from My Orders > Report an issue.

We investigate with the courier within 3–5 business days. If the courier can't show proof of delivery, we send a replacement or a full refund.

## Failed delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If all attempts fail, the order is returned to us. Prepaid orders are refunded in full, minus the shipping charge if one was paid.

To avoid failed deliveries, make sure your phone number is correct, add a landmark to your address, and keep your phone reachable on the delivery day.

## Repeated refused Cash on Delivery orders
If Cash on Delivery orders are refused at the door repeatedly, COD may be turned off for your account. See "Cash on Delivery" in our Payments policy.
