# Payments

## Payment methods we accept
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and others), credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express), net banking, popular wallets, and Cash on Delivery (COD). You can also pay fully or partly with UnoCX store credit, gift cards and loyalty points.

International credit and debit cards are accepted for orders delivered to an address in India. All prices are charged in Indian Rupees (INR); your bank may add a currency conversion fee.

## Prices and taxes
All prices on the website and app are in Indian Rupees and include GST. The price you pay is the price shown at checkout when you place the order. If a product's price changes after you order, the order price stays the same.

We don't offer price protection. If the price drops after you've ordered, you can cancel the order before dispatch and place a new one at the lower price.

## Cash on Delivery
Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to ₹5,000. A COD handling fee of ₹49 applies. COD is not available for some remote pincodes, for gift card purchases, or for customers who have refused two or more COD deliveries in the past. You can pay the courier in cash or by scanning their UPI QR code.

Please keep the exact amount ready if you pay in cash, as couriers may not carry change. For orders above ₹5,000, please pay online. The ₹49 COD fee is not refunded on returns, except for damaged, defective or wrong items.

## EMI
No-cost EMI is available on select credit cards for orders of ₹3,000 or more, for 3 or 6 months. Standard EMI is available on most credit cards. EMI options are shown at checkout.

With no-cost EMI, the interest is given to you as an upfront discount, so you pay only the product price over the EMI period. If you return an item bought on EMI, the refund goes to your card, and you should contact your bank to close or adjust the EMI. Your bank may charge a processing or foreclosure fee, which UnoCX can't refund.

## Payment failed but money was deducted
If your payment failed but money was deducted, don't worry. The amount is automatically reversed by your bank within 5–7 business days. If your order was confirmed despite the error, you will receive an order confirmation email. Do not pay again unless you want to place a new order.

If the money hasn't come back after 7 business days, contact our support team with the transaction reference (UTR) from your bank or UPI app.

## Charged twice for one order
If you were charged twice for the same order, the extra payment is detected automatically and refunded to the same payment method within 5–7 business days. You don't need to do anything. If you can't see the refund after that, contact our support team with both transaction references.

## Card or UPI payment declined
A payment can be declined by your bank for reasons such as a wrong OTP, a daily limit, insufficient balance, an expired card, or international transactions being turned off. Please try again, use another payment method, or contact your bank. UnoCX can't see the exact reason your bank declined a payment.

## Store credit
Store credit is added to your account when you choose it for a refund or a Cash on Delivery return. It is valid for 12 months from the date it's added, can be used on any order, and can be combined with a coupon and any other payment method. Store credit cannot be transferred to another account or withdrawn as cash.

## Gift cards as payment
You can use one or more UnoCX gift cards on an order and pay any balance with another method. Gift cards can't be used to buy other gift cards. See the FAQ for how gift cards work.

## Coupons and offers
Only one coupon can be used per order. Coupons cannot be applied to gift card purchases. If an order is partly returned, the coupon discount is adjusted proportionally in the refund. Current offers are shown on the Offers page and at checkout; our support team cannot create or extend coupons.

Each coupon has its own conditions, such as a minimum order value, eligible products, an expiry date, or first-order only. If a coupon doesn't apply, checkout shows the reason. Bank and card offers are given by the bank and may be credited as cashback by the bank after the order.

## Invoices
A GST invoice is emailed when your order is dispatched. You can also download it from My Orders > Invoice. To add a GSTIN, enter it at checkout; it cannot be added after the order is placed.

If an order ships in more than one package, you may receive one invoice per package. For returns, a credit note is issued with your refund.

## Payment security
UnoCX will never ask for your card number, CVV, OTP or UPI PIN by phone, chat, email or WhatsApp. If someone asks for these claiming to be from UnoCX, do not share them and report it to support.

All online payments are processed by RBI-authorised payment partners over encrypted connections. We do not store your full card number or CVV. If you see a payment on your statement that you don't recognise, contact your bank immediately and then contact our support team.
